Designed for Attorneys, Backed by Compliance

At Innovtelier, we provide law firms and licensed attorneys with reliable legal and organizational support tailored to your practice needs. From case file organization and discovery assistance to research and trial preparation support, our team integrates seamlessly into your workflow—always under attorney supervision and in full compliance with professional rules.

We require every client to designate a licensed supervising attorney in good standing who is responsible for reviewing and approving all work, in accordance with Rule 5.3.

We never communicate with an attorney's clients or outside parties, ensuring compliance with Rule 5.5. All services are designed to assist attorneys in their work, not replace attorney judgment or advice.
